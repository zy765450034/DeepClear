Privacy policy

Your files stay yours.

Purivo does not collect personal data. Analysis happens inside the folders you authorize through macOS.

What Purivo does not collect

Purivo does not collect, store, transmit, or share file names, paths, sizes, metadata, scan results, usage analytics, or account information.

How access works

The Mac App Store build uses App Sandbox and the standard macOS folder picker. Purivo stores a security-scoped bookmark and works only inside that scope. The direct build may request native macOS authorization for protected app bundles.

Deletion safety

Scanning never changes files. Selected files are moved to macOS Trash. Permanent cleanup requires an explicit confirmation and stays within the authorized scope.

隐私政策

你的文件，只属于你。

Purivo 不收集个人数据。所有分析都发生在你通过 macOS 授权的目录内。

Purivo 不收集什么

Purivo 不会收集、存储、传输或共享文件名、路径、大小、元数据、扫描结果、使用统计或账号信息。

目录访问如何工作

Mac App Store 版使用 App Sandbox 和 macOS 标准文件选择器。Purivo 保存安全作用域书签，只在授权范围内工作；直装版处理受保护应用包时可能请求 macOS 原生授权。

删除安全

扫描不会修改文件。选中的文件只会移入 macOS 废纸篓；永久清理必须经过明确确认，并且严格限制在授权范围内。

Support

Questions, answered.

A few practical notes for getting the most from Purivo.

Why does Purivo need folder access?
The Mac App Store sandbox requires you to explicitly choose the folder an app may access. Purivo stores that permission as a security-scoped bookmark.
What does the uninstaller remove?
It reviews the selected app bundle and recognizable user-level support files, preferences, caches, saved state, containers, and related launch-agent entries. You confirm the final Trash action.
Does Purivo send any data to the internet?
No. Purivo is entirely local and makes no network requests.
Which macOS versions are supported?
Purivo requires macOS 12 Monterey or later and supports both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
使用帮助

常见问题，都有答案。

几条实用说明，帮助你更好地使用 Purivo。

为什么 Purivo 需要目录权限？
Mac App Store 沙盒要求你明确选择应用可以访问的目录。Purivo 会将权限保存为安全作用域书签。
卸载应用会处理什么？
Purivo 会查看应用包以及用户级支持文件、偏好设置、缓存、保存状态、容器和相关启动项；你确认后才会移入废纸篓。
Purivo 会把数据上传到网络吗？
不会。Purivo 完全在本机运行，不会发起网络请求。
支持哪些 macOS 版本？
Purivo 需要 macOS 12 Monterey 或更高版本，同时支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel Mac。
